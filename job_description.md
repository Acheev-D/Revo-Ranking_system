# Job Description: Senior AI Engineer — Founding Team

**Company:** Redrob AI (Series A AI-native talent intelligence platform)  
**Location:** Pune / Noida, India (Hybrid) | Open to relocation candidates from Tier-1 Indian cities  
**Employment Type:** Full-time  
**Experience Required:** 5–9 years  

---

### Role Overview & Responsibilities
Own the intelligence layer of the product, including candidate-job matching, semantic retrieval, and ranking algorithms at scale.

* **System Architecture:** Design and deploy modern ML systems focusing on embeddings, hybrid retrieval (dense + sparse), and cross-encoder/LLM-based re-ranking.
* **Continuous Improvement:** Ship iterative improvements to ranking models that demonstrably enhance recruiter discovery and engagement metrics.
* **Evaluation Frameworks:** Design and maintain rigorous evaluation infrastructure (NDCG, MRR, MAP, offline benchmarks, and online A/B testing).
* **Scaling:** Drive the long-term technical roadmap for candidate matching across large-scale datasets and collaborate with product engineering.

---

### Skills & Requirements

#### Must-Have Core Qualifications:
* **Embeddings & Semantic Search:** Strong production experience with embeddings-based retrieval systems (e.g., Sentence-Transformers, BGE, E5, OpenAI embeddings). Experience managing embedding drift, index refreshes, and retrieval quality regression.
* **Vector Databases & Hybrid Search:** Production experience with vector search engines or hybrid search infrastructure (e.g., Pinecone, Weaviate, Qdrant, Milvus, OpenSearch, Elasticsearch, FAISS).
* **Programming & Systems:** Proficiency in Python with a focus on clean, high-performance, and maintainable production code.
* **Rigorous Evaluation:** Demonstrated experience developing and interpreting ranking evaluation metrics (NDCG, MRR, MAP, offline-to-online correlation, A/B testing).
* **Applied Machine Learning:** 5–9 years of experience with 4+ years dedicated to applied ML/AI systems deployed to real users.

#### Preferred / Nice-to-Have:
* Experience with LLM fine-tuning techniques (LoRA, QLoRA, PEFT).
* Practical experience with Learning-to-Rank (LTR) algorithms (e.g., LambdaMART, XGBoost, or neural rankers).
* Background in distributed systems or large-scale inference optimization.
* Prior experience in HR-tech, talent intelligence, or marketplace platforms.
* Contributions to open-source ML/AI projects.

---

### Disqualifiers & Non-Fits
* Pure academic or laboratory research backgrounds without real-world production deployment experience.
* AI experience limited strictly to simple API wrappers / tutorials (e.g., beginner LangChain scripts without underlying ML depth).
* Candidates who have transitioned away from writing production code in recent years.
* Pure computer vision, robotics, or speech backgrounds lacking NLP/IR (Information Retrieval) depth.

---

### Location & Logistics
* **Location:** Pune or Noida preferred (hybrid). Candidates in Tier-1 cities (Delhi NCR, Mumbai, Hyderabad, Bengaluru) open to relocation.
* **Notice Period:** Sub-30-day notice strongly preferred (notice buyout available).
